The Journey

How I got here

Three years, a design degree, two international awards, and a habit of not stopping at the Figma file. Click a year.

'21
Communication Design, Akademia Evolucion Where the obsession with type and hierarchy started.
Started a BA in Communication Design in Prishtina — visual communication, branding, typography, UI/UX, product design and multimedia. Three years of learning why things work before learning to make them look good. That order turned out to matter.
TypographyBrandingFoundations
'24
FormBrand, and an award I didn't expect The RrugeFest identity picked up two international recognitions.
Joined FormBrand as Visual & UI/UX Designer, designing interfaces for campaigns and merchandise, and building brand systems. The RrugeFest festival identity — built on adaptive typography, high-contrast colour and the theme "Infused Void" — earned an Honorable Mention at the IDA Design Awards and recognition from the World Brand Design Society. First time outside opinion confirmed what I was doing.
Brand IdentityCampaignIDA Award
'24
Started teaching at Cacttus Education Explaining the craft made me much better at it.
Became Visual/UI Design Trainer at Cacttus Education, teaching practical design workflows — UI, visual hierarchy, typography, layout and core principles. Nothing exposes a shaky opinion faster than a room of students asking why. Teaching forced me to turn instinct into reasoning I could defend.
TeachingUI DesignTypography
'25
Freelance, and real client pressure Clients across Switzerland and the Netherlands.
Freelance UI/UX work — restructuring outdated layouts into something more accessible and balanced, redesigning the NeoHome homepage for a Swiss real estate company, and helping a Netherlands-based client transform their salon website. Learning to balance what a client wants against what a user actually needs.
Web DesignE-commerceUI/UX
'26
Agilyti — designing and shipping Stopped handing off. Started building.
Joined Agilyti in April 2026, designing across the full company surface — website, product sections, brand assets, campaigns and editorial. This is where I stopped stopping at the Figma file: designing animated sections and taking them all the way to working, production-ready components. Same design instinct, much shorter distance between idea and live.
